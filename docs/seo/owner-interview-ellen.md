## Why I'm asking

We're updating the Cozelos Data website so it accurately represents what the company does today.

While reviewing the site, I found something worth pausing on. **The website and the company's federal registration describe two different businesses.**

The website sells web design, video, marketing, SEO, automation, and support.

The registration — the NAICS codes filed under CAGE 897W0 — describes a software and data-systems company: custom computer programming, computer systems design, computer facilities management, and software publishing.

I also found the earlier versions of the Cozelos Data website, going back to the Huntsville years. They describe a data management and analytics company with a full technical services line, employees, and its own software product.

And Google still associates cozelosdata.com with that older business. The domain currently ranks at or near **position 1** for searches like *software development services*, *IT services companies near me*, and *software engineering firm* — better than it ranks for anything the current site actually sells.

**None of this is a problem to fix. It might be an opportunity.** But I don't want to guess at it. Before changing anything, I need to understand the business from you.

A few ground rules so this is useful:

- There are **no wrong answers**. "We stopped doing that" is just as useful as "we still do that."
- I'm asking what the company **sells and can deliver today** — not what you're personally capable of. Those are different, and the difference matters a great deal for how we build the site.
- Nothing on the site changes until you've answered these.

## What happens after this

Once I have your answers, I'll be able to recommend how the website should be organized — which is the decision everything else is waiting on.

The realistic options look like this, and **your answers decide which one is honest**:

| If it turns out that… | Then the site should… |
|---|---|
| The technical work is occasional, and it's essentially you | Keep the six services, and strengthen the credentials section — no overclaiming |
| Data and software work is genuinely sold and deliverable | Add a proper second offering alongside the current services |
| Both sides are real businesses with different customers | Reorganize the site around the two audiences |
| Managed IT for local businesses is real | Add it as a **seventh local service** — there's demonstrated local demand for it |

I want to flag one thing plainly, because it affects what we can responsibly build.

Right now the site's technical and government credibility rests entirely on **your personal background**. There's no company project history on the site to support it, and no federal award record. For a local business hiring you for a website, that's fine. For a contracting officer or a prime contractor, personal experience and company past performance are two different things, and they will look for the second one.

That's not a reason to avoid the technical side. **It's a reason to build the proof before building the pages** — which is a very solvable problem, and one worth doing in the right order.
