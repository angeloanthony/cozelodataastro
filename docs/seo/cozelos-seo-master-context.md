# Part I — Positioning

## 1. The core strategic decision

Cozelos Data is **not** positioned as another web design company.

The positioning is **digital assets and growth systems**, not websites:

> **We Build Digital Assets That Grow Businesses.**
> **Making Businesses Stronger. Making Life Better.**
> Fast. Secure. Built to Rank.
> Custom engineered. You own everything.
> Business results instead of vanity metrics.

**Why this was right:** "web design in Vernal" is a commodity search category. "Digital assets that grow businesses" is a differentiated one. The commodity framing competes on price; the asset framing competes on outcome and supports the $2,500 floor.

This is now reflected throughout the live site — the homepage describes "complete digital systems" that generate leads, build authority, and create long-term growth.

> ⚠️ **Confirmed and strengthened 2026-08-12.** "Making Businesses Stronger & Life Better" is not new brand language — it is the **1998–2019 Huntsville tagline, unchanged**. It survived three rebuilds, two states, and a complete change of market. This is the single most durable element of the brand and should be treated as such.

# Part II — Market and audience

## 4. Geographic strategy

Primary entity: **Vernal, Utah.** Expansion rings: Uintah Basin → Eastern Utah → Utah → nationwide.

The site establishes Vernal as home base rather than pretending to be a generic national agency. The About page reinforces: headquartered in Vernal, invested in the Uintah Basin, serving clients nationwide, enterprise-grade engineering brought to Eastern Utah.

**The point is entity consistency, not keyword stuffing.** The value comes from a coherent, repeated, verifiable business identity — not from "Vernal Utah" in more headings.

## 5. The market is bigger than the city

Vernal itself is roughly 10,400 residents. The **Uintah Basin** is the real market.

So the model is not "how do we rank for 500 local searches in Vernal?" It is:

> Vernal + Uintah Basin + Eastern Utah + specialized industries + statewide/national expertise

That scales; pure-local does not.

## 6. Target customer groups

**Primary local:** contractors · hotels · tourism companies · healthcare practices · pest control · self-storage · restaurants · law firms · luxury services · small businesses

**Higher-value / institutional:** government · federal contracting · organizations requiring technical development · agencies needing technical subcontracting

The live homepage lists attorneys, medical professionals, contractors, tourism companies, government, and small businesses. The portfolio adds hotels, healthcare, storage, pest control, luxury services, and genealogy.

> ⚠️ **The second group is now blocked pending owner input.** See §29. Do not build content targeting federal/agency buyers until capability and capacity are confirmed.

1. Website Design & Development

6. Ongoing Support & Maintenance

Presented deliberately as **one connected growth system**, not six unrelated services:

# Part VI — Entity and authority

## 18. Founder entity SEO

The strategy is not to rank a company name. It is to build a connected entity graph:

```
Ellen Cozelos → Cozelos Data → Vernal
    → software engineering → cybersecurity → AI
    → digital marketing → SEO
```

Published entity attributes: software engineer · cybersecurity specialist · AI professional · digital strategist · 20+ years · NASA · Department of Defense · aerospace · commercial programs · Columbia University.

> ⚠️ **Two items unverified — do not reinforce until confirmed.**
> **(a) Degree level.** The site claims a **Master's from Columbia**, including in `Person` schema `alumniOf`. The 2019 company site stated **B.S. Computer Science, Columbia** plus **B.S. Economics, University of Alabama in Huntsville**. Seven years is ample time to have earned a Master's — but it is asserted in structured data on government-facing pages and must be confirmed.
> **(b) The framing is weaker than the record.** The documented 2019 bio — *"20-year veteran of supporting and leading the development of large, complex data systems as a contractor to the federal government including the U.S. Army, NASA, and Army Corps of Engineers,"* in the roles Database Developer, Software Engineer, Task Lead, Project Lead — is **more specific, more checkable, and better aligned to the registered NAICS codes** than the current "NASA / DoD / aerospace" framing. The rebrand traded a defensible claim for a vaguer, grander one. Recommend reverting once confirmed.

## 20. Government authority cluster

Currently published: WOSB · SAM-ready positioning · DUNS · CAGE 897W0 · NAICS 513210/541511/541512/541513 · Section 508 · capability statement on request.

The proposed cluster was: government web development · federal web development · Section 508 web development · government SEO · government website accessibility · federal AI tooling · government automation · WOSB technology contractor.

> ⚠️ **This entire cluster is blocked.** Three findings from 2026-08-12:
> 1. **No federal past performance exists.** USAspending (authoritative, no opt-out) shows zero prime contracts, zero IDVs, zero subawards, 2007→present. A government cluster would rest entirely on the founder's résumé — and personal experience is not company past performance.
> 2. **The SAM registration is not publicly discoverable.** A public search for "cozelos" returns one unrelated HHS exclusion; CAGE 897W0 returns nothing. Entities may opt out of public display, so this is *not* proof of lapse — but it is the wall a contracting officer hits.
> 3. **The site publishes a DUNS but no UEI**, while `Credentials.astro` advertises "DUNS / UEI Active." DUNS was retired for federal award purposes in April 2022.
>
> **Sequence matters here: build the proof before building the pages.** A government hub with no past performance actively reduces trust with the buyer it targets.

# Part VII — Differentiators

## 21. Speed

Claims: sub-second load times · custom engineering · lean architecture · no bloated templates · strong Lighthouse scores. The homepage presents a 0.9s figure and a Lighthouse card.

The argument: speed → better UX → better engagement → better conversion → stronger overall site quality.

## 22. "You own everything"

Client owns: domain · hosting account · source code · content. No proprietary builder, no lock-in, no hostage retainer.

Not directly an SEO factor, but it answers the real objection — *"what happens if I stop using the agency?"* — and it is a genuine trust differentiator against typical local providers.

# Part IX — What changed on 2026-08-12

## 27. What the investigation found

A GSC page-filtered export showed the domain ranking at **position 1** for `software development services`, `it companies near me`, `it services companies near me`, and `software engineering firm` — on a legacy URL (`/?page_id=158`), not on any current page. Tracing that produced four independent evidence sources that converge:

**The company has a real, documented prior identity as a data-systems firm.**

| Era | Location | What it was |
|---|---|---|
| ≤2019 | **Huntsville, AL** | Data Management & Analytics firm **with employees**: Data Management (custom software, database design, MDM, governance; solution set **MASTERMIND**) · Managed IT Services (18 line items, flat-rate, 24/7) · IT Staff (augmentation) |
| 2020–21 | Huntsville, AL | WordPress rebuild; Data Management + App Development; **Perseus** in nav |
| 2022–mid-2025 | **Vernal, UT** | *"Needing Expert Software Engineers?"* — MBSE · DoDAF/UPDM · RMF/CMMC · Cyber Range-in-the-Box · ANN. **Oracle Partner** link |
| mid-2025→ | Vernal, UT | Current Astro marketing site — six local digital services |

Supporting facts:

- **The registration describes the old business, not the current one.** NAICS 541511 (primary), 541512, 541513, 513210 map word-for-word onto the old Company page. **None of the six services the site sells today are covered by them** — advertising (541810), video (512110), and marketing consulting (541613) are not filed.
- **The technical side was not just a résumé.** The 2019 careers page recruited SQL Server developers and DBAs, C#/VB programmers, Microsoft-certified technicians, and cybersecurity technicians, with a 401(k) and full benefits, plus an employee intranet, time-and-expense, and retirement portal.
- **NAICS 513210 was real.** MASTERMIND → **PerseusDB**, a genuine SaaS for independent insurance agents ($29.95/mo, Stripe billing, AWS Cognito auth, named testimonials). **The domain stopped resolving around July 2025.**
- **No federal award history exists.** USAspending: zero, all classes, 2007→present.
- **The current business is website assets** — sites built to bring clients and grow the client's business, with the other five services attaching around that core.

## 28. What this does and does not change

**Does not change:** the positioning, the brand architecture, the local strategy, pillars 1–4, the portfolio opportunity, the content roadmap, or the "two projects" model. The strategy in Parts I–VIII stands.

**Does change:**
- The six services are an **anchor plus attachments**, not six equals (§7)
- Pillar 5 and the government cluster are **frozen** pending owner answers (§13, §20)
- The founder entity claims need **verification**, and the older framing is stronger (§18)
- The legacy rankings are **historical search equity, not a strategic directive** — evidence that the domain has an association, not proof of demand. 17 exposed impressions; position 1 at 1–3 impressions is association, not market demand
