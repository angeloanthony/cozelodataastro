Why Choose Cozelos Data?

Cozelos is a locally owned and operated Data Management and Analytics company committed to the success of our clients, our employees and our community. We work hard to be the best we can be technically, as a company, and as residents of our local area.



Why Choose Cozelos Data?

We listen to our clients, set clear expectations, offer no-risk contracts, provide a complete QA process, and support our clients for as long as they need us.

We work as a partner with our clients with transparency and open, honest communication throughout the development life-cycle. Our clients depend on us to listen to them and evolve with them as a trusted business partner.

Our client’s data systems are critical to their business and when managed correctly provide a competitive edge in the marketplace. Our goal is 100% up time of our client’s systems and if something does go wrong, we are available 24/7 to get back to full functionality.
