Data Management
Development Life-Cycle

Client decision point

Assess:

In the assess phase our experts meet with the client to conduct an initial needs assessment and identification of high-level requirements. These requirements are documented and agreed upon then used to develop a proposal for creating the desired solution. The proposal is presented to the client for approval and signature and includes:

  • Requirements list
  • Assumptions
  • Deliverables
  • Schedule
  • Cost
  • Expected benefits

Design:

During the design phase, our experts work closely with the client’s staff to define detailed requirements and create an efficient, cost-effective design. The design will address:

  • Functionality
  • Processes
  • Interfaces
  • Input/Output
  • Relationships
  • Accessibility
  • Security
  • Configuration

Upon completion of the design it will be reviewed and approved by the client before proceeding to the build phase.

Build:

During the build phase, the design is coded, components are integrated, and the systems is fully tested. A completed set of documentation is produced in this phase as well. The client is included in weekly build reviews and can make adjustments and provide feedback throughout the build process. This ensures a better-quality product and customer satisfaction with the delivered solution. The client participates in a readiness review at the end of this phase and approves the system before installation begins.

Install:

During the install phase the system is compiled, configured, installed, and tested. Any data transformation, migration, or creation is performed, and any system orientation or training is conducted. At the end of this phase the system is fully operational, and the client participates in an acceptance review before signing off on the delivered system. Upon accepting delivery of the system, the remaining 50% of the price is due.

Maintain:

The maintenance phase is a subscription service to acquire ongoing support for the delivered system including:

  • System audit
  • Patches and updates
  • Backup and recovery
  • Security maintenance
  • Continuity of operations
